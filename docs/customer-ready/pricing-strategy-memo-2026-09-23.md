## 1. The decision

We will not sell UM as a single per-auth fee, and we will not give the engine away. Two lines:

- **Platform / access — $1.50 PMPM** (band $1.25–$1.75, floor $1.25). Covers the engine, criteria, intake rails, gold-card file, reporting.
- **Clinical review — only when a human opens the chart.** Nurse $85 · Physician $200 · External $350. Auto-approvals itemize at $0.

Auto is a client benefit, not a loss leader. If auto-rate rises — which is the product working — platform holds and review fees step down by contract. We do not get paid to stay manual.

## 5. Why two lines

A pure per-case shop dies if the AI works. Every +10 points of auto-rate drops ~$7–9M of review revenue and only ~$1M of cost. Platform does not move. That is the hedge.

A pure PMPM shop hides auto vs. nurse vs. MD and looks like every other UM vendor. Two lines let a CFO see labor, and let a TPA white-label without defending a black box.

UM is a product. It does not fit inside a $12 PEPM all-in TPA admin fee. Claims, network, IDR, and credentialing stay on a separate admin line.

## 7. Contract guardrails we will publish

Auto itemized at $0 every month. Auto-rate published quarterly. Gold-card = no review fee. Highest touch billed once — no stacked nurse + MD + external on one case. Quotes cap modeled MD + external at 12% of inbound so worst case is bounded. AI may auto-approve against locked criteria; AI may not deny medical necessity without a clinician.

Review-fee step-down as trailing-90-day auto-rate rises (platform does not step down):

| Trailing auto-rate | Nurse | Physician | External |
|--|--|--|--|
| 50–59% | $85 | $200 | $350 |
| 60–69% | $75 | $185 | $330 |
| ≥ 70% | $70 | $175 | $315 |

## 9. Go-to-market

Buyer: the self-funded employer, or the TPA that already owns the group. TPA white-label is the faster channel. We do not ask a TPA to give up claims, network, or stop-loss.

Broker conversation is PEPM. CFO conversation is PMPM. Never mix unlabeled denominators.

Client-facing sentence: “You pay a small platform fee for the engine. You pay a review fee only when a clinician opens the chart. Auto-approvals are $0.”

Do not lead external materials with $54.6M. Lead with the fee table and the 375k autos at $0.

## 10. Risks and asks

Inbound is an assumption. A 400k live book cuts review revenue to ~$24M and planning profit to ~$6–8M plus platform. First build task is a service-category inbound file, not nurse hiring.

The family factor is tight. 1.50 lives/EE vs. typical 2.0–2.4. If the book is 2.2, PMPM falls and PEPM does not. Lock the denominator before anyone quotes.

Criteria licenses are required COGS, not optional. Get a vendor quote before we treat $24M as a forecast.

Asks. Team: lock the price card in config; invoice engine posts auto rows at $0; dashboard shows inbound / auto / nurse / MD / external / first-pass / billed PEPM / billed PMPM. Advisors: pressure-test the $1.50 platform and the step-down schedule against what mid-market brokers will actually take.

## 11. What we will not do

Make auto a loss leader. Charge a review fee on auto. Bundle this UM into $12 PEPM all-in admin. Quote PEPM and PMPM as the same number. Build capacity for 285k annual nurse reviews before inbound is proven. Let a rising auto-rate look like a broken forecast.
